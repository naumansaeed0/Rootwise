Reading the signals

Light bars show relative light level. Water bars summarize moisture guidance, never days between watering. Care bars show difficulty. Every meter includes a text label.

✓Pet-friendly!Toxic / irritating?Unverified

Pet-friendly means listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs, not edible. Any plant can cause stomach upset if eaten. Unverified plants are excluded from that filter. Read our safety notes.
