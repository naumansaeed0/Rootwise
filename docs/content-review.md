# Plant content wording review

Reviewed on 19 November 2026 for the final polish round. The plant descriptions and guides were written for Rootwise from factual research notes, not imported from source articles. No copied description or guide passage was identified, and no passage required rewriting in this round.

## Checks performed

- Reviewed the wording and provenance of all **55 plant descriptions** and **six evergreen guides**, including their intros and article sections.
- Compared those texts and all plant care fields (**525 fields/sections in total**) against paragraphs from **63 accessible cited source URLs**.
- Normalized words and checked for runs of eight or more identical consecutive words and unusually similar paragraphs (similarity threshold 0.68).
- No potential copied/closely matching passage met either threshold. The longest identical word run was **six words**, consisting of ordinary care terminology. Botanical names, factual care concepts, and short standard terms naturally overlap.
- No cited-source photographs, graphics, website layout, or source article text is shipped as a Rootwise asset. Source excerpts used for the review stayed outside the repository; only the review summary and URLs are retained.

The machine-readable summary is [audits/content-review.json](audits/content-review.json). Cultivation and safety sources are listed in [data-review.md](data-review.md) and each plant record.

## Limits

A comparison with accessible source paragraphs is not a legal originality certificate or an exhaustive search of everything published online. The check supports the narrower statement that **no copied passages were found in the reviewed descriptions/guides against the cited material**. Factual accuracy, toxicity verification, and originality are separate questions; the nine existing botanical verification notes remain unchanged.
