# Botanical data review

The library contains **55 profiles in seven categories**. Care fields are practical guidance, not cultivar-specific prescriptions. General watering, soil, feeding, repotting, and problem-solving paragraphs are shared by appropriate care groups. Descriptions, taxonomy, origins, propagation, and relatives are separately curated. All 55 primary source URLs were reachable during the source review on 19 November 2026. Reachability alone is not verification of every fact.

## Safety policy

The three species with unconfirmed cat/dog safety—Chinese money plant, prayer plant, and maidenhair fern—are labeled **Unverified**, not non-toxic. The pet-friendly filter excludes them. Non-toxic does not mean edible. Other profile verification notes describe scope or identity uncertainty, rather than reversing a known toxicity warning.

Bird of paradise and purple heart are conservatively labeled toxic/irritating following NC State’s species warnings. Bird’s nest fern cites Clemson Extension for its non-toxic classification. Money tree and moth orchid include direct ASPCA references. Most other pet labels use explicit NC State species warnings or non-toxic cat/dog tags. No conclusion is inferred from an absent warning.

## Editorial limits

The library is broad introductory reference content, not a botanical monograph. It does not claim a professional horticultural, toxicological, or taxonomic review. Generic care ranges, size groups, and difficulty labels are editorial estimates. Hybrid identity and nursery names remain especially prone to ambiguity. All references are linked; third-party photos and long source text are not copied.
